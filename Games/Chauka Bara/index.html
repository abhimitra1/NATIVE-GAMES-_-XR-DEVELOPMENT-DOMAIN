Chowka Bhara — The Traditional Cowrie Race of Destiny

📜 ABOUT THE GAME

Chauka Bara (also known as Chowka Bhara, Ashta Chamma, or Kavade Kali) is one of India's oldest strategic race games, played on a sacred square grid with natural cowrie shells as dice.

✨ ANCIENT HERITAGE

Played for centuries across Indian households, combining tactical movement, blocking, risk management, and cowrie probability!

Four natural cowrie shells are thrown on the square tray. Count the Mouth-Up shells:

4 Up = 4 Steps ✨ Bonus Roll
0 Up = 8 Steps ✨ Bonus Roll
1 Up = 1 Step
2 Up = 2 Steps
3 Up = 3 Steps
🛡️
Safe Cross Squares (X):

Squares with cross hatchings are sanctuaries. Multiple pawns from any player can share safe squares without risk.

💥
Capturing Pawns:

Landing on an unprotected opponent pawn captures it, sending it back to start and earning a Bonus Roll!

Tokens race counter-clockwise around outer rings before turning inward to the central HOME house.

🏆 First player to get all 4 pawns inside HOME wins!

🛡️ ✕

Safe Havens (Cross Squares)

Squares marked with a cross (✕) are 100% Safe. Pawns resting here cannot be captured, and multiple pawns can stack together safely.

⚔️ 💥

Enemy Pawn Capture

Landing on an opponent's pawn on an unmarked square captures it, sending it back to start and granting you a Bonus Throw!

🌟
Golden Rule 1: Always seek shelter in Safe Squares (✕) if an enemy pawn is lurking behind you!
🎲
Golden Rule 2: Rolling an 8 or 4 awards a Bonus Throw — roll again before selecting which pawn to move!
🏆
Golden Rule 3: Capturing an opponent grants an extra roll — aggressive play pays off!
