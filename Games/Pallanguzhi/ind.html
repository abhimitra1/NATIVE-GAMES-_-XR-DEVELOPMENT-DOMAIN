How to Play

  • Choose a cup containing seeds.
  • Pick up all seeds from that cup.
  • Drop one seed into each following cup.
  • Continue sowing around the board.
  • If the last seed lands in an empty cup on your side, capture the opposite seeds.
  • When a player has no seeds available at the beginning of their turn, the round ends.
