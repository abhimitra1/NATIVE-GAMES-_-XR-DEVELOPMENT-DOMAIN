📖 ASHTAA CHEMMAA RULES

OVERVIEW

Ashtaa Chemmaa (also known as Chauka Bara, Katte Mane, or Daayam) is an ancient Indian cross-and-circle board game dating back centuries. It is the tactical ancestor of modern Ludo and Pachisi.

THE 5x5 BOARD

The board consists of 25 squares. The outer ring contains 16 squares, the inner ring contains 8 squares, and the center square (3,3) is the goal. Each player has 4 pieces.

SAFE SQUARES (GADDI)

The squares marked with ornate crosses (Red: 3,1; Yellow: 1,3; Blue: 3,5; Green: 5,3) and the Center Goal (3,3) are Safe Squares. Pieces on safe squares cannot be captured, and multiple pieces can safely rest there.

COWRIE SHELL SCORING

Four cowrie shells are tossed on each turn:

  • 0 Open (All closed): ASHTA! 8 moves + EXTRA TURN!
  • 4 Open (All open): CHAMMA! 4 moves + EXTRA TURN!
  • 1 Open: 1 move
  • 2 Open: 2 moves
  • 3 Open: 3 moves

ENTERING THE BOARD

A piece in reserve can ONLY enter the track when you roll an ASHTA (8) or CHAMMA (4)! It enters onto your home safe square and you get an extra throw.

CAPTURING (VETTU)

When you land on an opponent's piece on any regular (unsafe) square, that piece is captured and sent back to their home reserve! Capturing also grants you an extra roll.

WINNING

The first player to navigate all 4 pieces through the outer perimeter, spiral into the inner track, and reach the Center Goal (3,3) with an exact roll wins!
