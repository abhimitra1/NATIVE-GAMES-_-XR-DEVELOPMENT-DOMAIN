How to Play

  • Herd places pieces on empty points. Once all 15 pieces are placed, the herd can move along the lines.
  • Tigers move along connected lines to empty points.
  • Tigers can jump over a herd piece to capture it when the next point is empty.
  • Tigers win after 6 captures.
  • The herd wins by blocking the tigers so they cannot make a legal move.
