Tiger and Goats

Traditional Tiger and Goats Strategy Game

📖 How to Play

  • There is 1 Tiger and 20 Goats.
  • At the beginning, click empty points to place goats.
  • Click the Tiger to select it.
  • The Tiger can move to an adjacent empty point.
  • The Tiger can capture a goat by jumping over it to an empty point.
  • After all goats are placed, select a goat and move it one step.
  • Tiger wins after capturing 5 goats.
  • Goats win when the Tiger has no legal move.
