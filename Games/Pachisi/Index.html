GAME PLAY GUIDE

1. Objective & Setup

Pachisi is a traditional Indian cross-and-circle board game.

  • Objective: Be the first player to navigate all 4 pawns around the board into the central home nest (Charkoni).
  • Players: Played with 2, 3, or 4 players (or AI bots).
  • Each player controls 4 colored pawns originating from their respective arm.

2. Cowrie Shell Scores

Movement points are determined by casting 6 cowrie shells:

  • 2 Open Shells: Move 2 Steps
  • 3 Open Shells: Move 3 Steps
  • 4 Open Shells: Move 4 Steps
  • 0 Open Shells: Move 6 Steps + Bonus Roll
  • 1 Open Shell: Move 10 Steps + Bonus Roll
  • 5 Open Shells: Move 25 Steps + Bonus Roll

3. Pawn Movement & Paths

Rules for navigating board paths:

  • Pawns travel counter-clockwise around the outer perimeter of the cruciform board.
  • After completing a circuit, pawns turn upward into their home column.
  • An exact score is required to land in the central Charkoni nest.

4. Safe Castles & Capturing

Interacting with opponent pawns:

  • Safe Squares: Cells marked with ✕ are Castles. Pawns here cannot be captured.
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent pawn on a regular cell captures it, sending it back to start!

5. Strategy & Winning

Mastering the game tactics:

  • Keep pawns grouped on Safe Squares when opponents approach.
  • Utilize high scores (10 or 25) to move pawns safely into home paths.
  • First player to place all 4 pawns into the center wins!
