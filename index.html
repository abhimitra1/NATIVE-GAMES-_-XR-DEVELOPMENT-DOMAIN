01 / THE LEGACY

Not relics.
Living traditions.

For generations, these games have travelled without boxes or rulebooks—passed from elder to child, scratched into stone, drawn with chalk and played with seeds, shells and stones.

This collection brings those acts of play to a new generation without sanding away what makes them distinct. Each game draws from An Anthology of Native & Indigenous Sports of India, translating documented rules and cultural context into an accessible digital experience.

03 / WHY THESE GAMES MATTER

Play that stays
with you.

Simple materials can hold remarkably deep ideas. These games turn every move into a small exercise in attention, imagination and togetherness.

  • ✦

    Think ahead

    Build foresight, logic, pattern recognition and tactical decision-making.

  • ◌

    Learn through play

    Practise counting, probability, memory and problem-solving without a worksheet.

  • ⌁

    Gather together

    Encourage conversation, patience, turn-taking and play across generations.

  • ◇

    Keep culture moving

    Meet regional names, materials and stories through direct, respectful participation.
